## 2024-03-05 - Accessibility for Configuration Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** During the review of the configuration components (`Variant` configurations, file listings), we observed that many icon-only buttons (download, remove, move up/down, delete mappings/rules) were lacking descriptive ARIA labels (`aria-label`) and explicit keyboard focus indicators (`focus-visible:ring-2` styling with appropriate colors). These interactions are critical for navigating complex forms without a mouse, and default browser outlines often fall short or do not align well with dark UI styling.
**Action:** When working on complex nested lists (like mappings, rules, variants, uploads) with action icons (trash, download, arrows), always verify that they have an explicitly descriptive `aria-label` specifying *what* they affect (e.g., "Delete Variant 1"), and apply explicit keyboard focus styling.
